## Why Choose HIIT?

HIIT workouts pack maximum results into minimal time. By alternating between intense bursts of effort and short recovery periods, you elevate your heart rate, boost metabolic rate, and build cardiovascular fitness — all in 20–30 minutes. No fancy equipment needed, just your bodyweight.

GymScript's built-in interval timers eliminate guesswork. The app counts down your work and rest periods, plays audio cues so you know when to switch, and keeps you on pace without checking your phone every few seconds. Focus on moving hard; let GymScript handle the timing.
