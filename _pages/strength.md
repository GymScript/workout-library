## Structured Strength Training

Building strength requires progressive overload and precise rest periods. A solid strength programme balances compound movements (squats, deadlifts, rows, presses) with targeted accessory work, spread across multiple sessions per week to allow recovery between muscle groups.

Paste any workout script into GymScript, and the app automatically parses sets, reps, and rest intervals. Hit "Start" and follow the timers — no need to count reps or watch the clock between sets. Track your progress session to session and watch your numbers climb.
